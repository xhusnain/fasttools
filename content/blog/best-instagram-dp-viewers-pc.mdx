## 2. Browser Extensions (The "Riskier" Option)

There are dozens of Chrome extensions like "IG Zoom" or "Downloader for Instagram."

### Pros:
*   **Convenient:** Adds a button directly to the Instagram website.

### Cons:
*   **Security Risk:** Many of these extensions require "Read/Write" access to your browser data. They can technically track your browsing history or steal cookies.
*   **Broken Often:** Instagram constantly updates its code to break these extensions. You'll often find they stop working after a few weeks.
*   **Banned Accounts:** Using unauthorized extensions while logged in can flag your Instagram account for "automated behavior," leading to shadowbans.

**Verdict:** Good for power users who understand the risks, but not recommended for the average person.

## 3. The "Right-Click + Inspect" Method (For Coders)

If you know HTML/CSS, you can do this manually.

1.  Right-click the profile picture.
2.  Select **"Inspect"**.
3.  Search the DOM for the image URL.

### Pros:
*   No tools required.

### Cons:
*   **Technical:** Requires understanding web code.
*   **Low Quality:** Instagram often serves a 320px version on desktop to save bandwidth. Finding the 1080px URL in the code is difficult because it's dynamic.

**Verdict:** Too slow and complicated for most users.
