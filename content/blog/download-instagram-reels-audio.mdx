## Why Audio Gets Lost

To understand the solution, you need to understand how Instagram delivers video. Instagram uses a technology called **DASH (Dynamic Adaptive Streaming over HTTP)**.

In simple terms, Instagram splits a Reel into two separate files:
1.  **The Video Track:** Just the moving images.
2.  **The Audio Track:** Just the sound.

Your phone combines them in real-time while you watch. When you use a basic downloader or screen recorder, it often only grabs the video track, ignoring the separate audio file.
