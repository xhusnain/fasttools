## 2. ExchangeRate-API
**Best for:** High availability needs.

A solid "freemium" option. The free tier is generous (1,500 requests/month) and supports 160+ currencies. It requires an email sign-up to easier management.

## 3. Open Exchange Rates
**Best for:** Enterprise-grade data.

One of the oldest players. The free plan is limited but provides hourly updates. It's an industry standard for a reason, but can be overkill for small projects.

## 4. Fixer.io
**Best for:** European market data.

Known for its high accuracy with EU data sources. The free plan is limited to 1,000 requests/month and base currency is restricted to EUR on the free tier, which can be a blocker for some.

## 5. CurrencyFreaks
**Best for:** Crypto support.

If you need fiat AND crypto rates in one place, this is a distinct choice. It supports hundreds of cryptocurrencies alongside traditional money.
