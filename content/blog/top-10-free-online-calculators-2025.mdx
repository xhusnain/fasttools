### The Science of Energy Expenditure

The "Calories In, Calories Out" (CICO) model follows the laws of thermodynamics, but the "Calories Out" part is highly variable:

<GChecklist items={[

The calculator must use the **Mifflin-St Jeor Equation**, widely recognized as the most accurate for the general population:

## 3. Smart Body Metrics Dashboard (BMI+)

**Primary Need:** Health Monitoring | **2025 Feature:** Relative Fat Mass (RFM) + Risk Assessment

The traditional Body Mass Index (BMI) is increasingly viewed as flawed, particularly for athletic populations or those undergoing rapid weight loss. However, "BMI Calculator" remains a massive search volume keyword.

### Moving Beyond BMI

The medical community in 2025 acknowledges BMI's utility as a **population screening tool** but its failure as an **individual diagnostic**. With the rise of "Skinny Fat" (Sarcopenic Obesity) awareness, users want body composition insights.

### The Multi-Metric Approach

Instead of standalone BMI, a "Body Metrics Dashboard" provides:

<GChecklist items={[
    "**BMI Calculation**: Standard height/weight metric for baseline reference",
    "**Relative Fat Mass (RFM)**: Newer, more accurate estimator using height and waist circumference",
    "**Waist-to-Height Ratio**: Simple but powerful health risk indicator",
    "**Lean Body Mass (Boer Formula)**: Muscle mass estimation"
]} />

### RFM Formula (More Accurate Than BMI)

The Relative Fat Mass index, validated by Cedar-Sinai researchers:

- **Men**: `64 - (20 × (Height / Waist))`
- **Women**: `76 - (20 × (Height / Waist))`

### Nuanced Interpretation

The dashboard provides context: *"Your BMI indicates 'Overweight' (26), but your Waist-to-Height ratio indicates 'Healthy' (0.48), suggesting high muscle mass."*

This nuance is exactly what users searching "Is BMI accurate" are looking for.
