# Perplexity Optimization - Quick Start Summary

## What We're Doing

Optimizing all FastTools pages to rank in Perplexity AI search based on reverse-engineered ranking factors from Perplexity's browser infrastructure.

## Why It Matters

- **Perplexity is growing:** More users are using AI search
- **Topic multipliers favor us:** AI/tech/science tools get exponential visibility
- **Early mover advantage:** Few sites are optimizing for Perplexity yet
- **Long-term traffic:** AI search is the future of information discovery

## Key Ranking Factors (Top 10)

1. **Answer-First Structure** (≤80 tokens + Because with number)
2. **Definition/Steps/X-vs-Y Blocks** (Three intent blocks)
3. **Entity Disambiguation** (Clear term definitions)
4. **Numerate Passages** (Concrete numbers throughout)
5. **Topic Multipliers** (AI/tech/science favored)
6. **New Post CTR Window** (Critical first minutes)
7. **Time Decay** (Requires regular refreshes)
8. **Embedding Similarity** (Semantic richness)
9. **Memory Networks** (Interconnected content)
10. **Authoritative References** (Link to GitHub, Stack Overflow, etc.)
