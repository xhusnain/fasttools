The Modern SEO Playbook: Your Step-by-Step Guide
This guide breaks down SEO into three phases: Planning, Execution, and Optimization.
Phase 1: Planning and Research
Before you write a single line of code or content, you must understand your audience.
Step 1: Define the Search Intent
Ask yourself: "Why is a person searching for this specific keyword?"
Query Example	Intent Type	What content to create?
unit converter	Navigational/Tool	A functional calculator page (like yours).
how many feet in a meter	Informational	A blog post/guide with context, formulas, and history.
buy conversion software	Transactional	A sales/download page for your app.
Action: Ensure your content matches the user's intent perfectly. A calculator page should focus on the tool itself, not a 2,000-word blog post.
Step 2: Keyword Research
Find the words and phrases your audience uses.
Action: Use a keyword research tool (like Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs, Semrush) to find relevant keywords with decent search volume.
Action: Focus on "long-tail keywords" (e.g., "celsius to fahrenheit conversion calculator") as they are less competitive and often have higher intent.
Action: Avoid creating massive keyword lists in a meta tag. Just keep a small list for your own reference during the next steps.
