# Backlink Monitoring Script for FastTools YouTube Tools

## Daily Monitoring Tasks

### 1. Google Search Console Check
- Check for new impressions and clicks for:
  - "youtube downloader" 
  - "youtube playlist downloader"
  - "fasttools youtube downloader"
- Monitor overall site performance
- Note any significant changes

### 2. Google Alerts Review
Set up Google Alerts for:
- "fasttools youtube downloader"
- "youtube downloader tool"
- "best youtube downloader 2026"
- "comprehensive guide to youtube downloading"

### 3. Social Media Monitoring
- Check for mentions of FastTools or our YouTube tools
- Monitor for shares of our new guide content
- Engage with any organic mentions

## Weekly Monitoring Tasks

### 1. Backlink Analysis
Using Ahrefs or similar tool:
- Check for new referring domains
- Analyze quality of new backlinks
- Monitor anchor text distribution
- Track competitor backlink activity

### 2. Keyword Ranking Check
Track rankings for:
- "youtube downloader" (position)
- "youtube playlist downloader" (position) 
- "fasttools youtube downloader" (position)
- "best youtube downloader" (position)
- "safe youtube downloader" (position)

### 3. Referral Traffic Analysis
In Google Analytics:
- Check referral traffic from outreach targets
- Analyze bounce rate and engagement
- Monitor conversion from referral traffic
- Track which sites drive the most valuable traffic

## Monthly Monitoring Tasks

### 1. Comprehensive Backlink Report
- Total referring domains
- Domain authority of linking sites
- Anchor text analysis
- Competitor comparison

### 2. Content Performance Review
- Page views for the comprehensive guide
- Time on page and engagement metrics
- Social shares and organic reach
- Comments and user feedback

### 3. ROI Assessment
- Cost of outreach effort vs. value received
- Quality of traffic from new backlinks
- Conversion rate from referral traffic
- Overall impact on search rankings

## Alert Triggers

### Immediate Attention Needed
- Sudden drop in rankings for target keywords
- Significant increase in referral traffic
- New backlinks from very high DA sites
- Negative mentions or reviews

### Weekly Review Required
- New backlinks from medium to high DA sites
- Changes in search position for target keywords
- New mentions in relevant communities
- Feedback from outreach contacts

## Reporting Schedule

### Weekly Reports
- Backlink acquisition summary
- Keyword ranking changes
- Referral traffic metrics
- Outreach campaign progress

### Monthly Reports
- Comprehensive backlink analysis
- ROI assessment of link building efforts
- Competitive analysis update
- Strategy refinement recommendations

## Tools Required

### Primary Tools
- Google Search Console
- Google Analytics
- Ahrefs or SEMrush (for backlink tracking)
- Google Alerts

### Secondary Tools
- Social media monitoring tools
- Keyword tracking software
- Website audit tools
- Competitor analysis platforms

## Success Metrics

### Primary Metrics
- Number of new referring domains per month
- Domain authority of new linking sites
- Improvement in target keyword rankings
- Increase in referral traffic

### Secondary Metrics
- Brand mentions without links
- Social shares of content
- Engagement with outreach content
- Quality of traffic from new links

## Adjustment Indicators

### Positive Indicators
- Increase in referring domains
- Higher quality backlinks (higher DA)
- Improved keyword rankings
- Increased referral traffic
- Positive engagement with content

### Negative Indicators
- No response to outreach
- Decrease in rankings
- Low engagement with content
- Spammy-looking backlinks
- High bounce rate from referral traffic

This monitoring system will help track the effectiveness of our link building campaign and allow for adjustments to maximize results.
