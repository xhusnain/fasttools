  **private-account and HD-download variants massively outperform** the generic head terms.
- Brand clicks stable — returning users navigate by brand, not bookmarks.

## What the data says (raw conclusions, expanded in 04-plan)

1. ~150 of 194 tools have **zero** GSC presence — not low, zero.
2. The IG cluster wins on **narrow high-intent variants** (1080p, private, HD)
   and loses on generic heads — classic low-authority + high-relevance pattern
   (exactly the Quaid "narrower index" thesis).
3. Two pages sit on large impressions with sub-1% CTR (insta dp viewer intents,
   channel checker) = biggest fastest wins available.
4. Question-shaped queries (future age, monthsary, "how long together") get
   impressions with no tool page winning — needs answer-first content pages.
5. Some matched queries are undesirable (seed-phrase-with-balance, leaked ghp_
   tokens). These attract scam-seekers and are trust/quality liabilities.
