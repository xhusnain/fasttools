## Search Intent

The dominant intent is mixed informational plus tool-seeking:

- People want to hide text, a secret message, or data inside an image.
- They often need a free online steganography tool.
- They need to know whether PNG or JPG is safer.
- They want to decode the hidden message later.
- More technical readers search for LSB steganography and password encryption.

SERP pattern observed on 2026-06-02:

- Tool-first pages such as Stegg, steg.fun, TextStudio, MYRQAI Stealth Lab, Universal Encoder Decoder, and similar image steganography tools.
- Repeated claims around LSB steganography, PNG output, password protection, and browser-based privacy.
- Opportunity for FastTools: combine a clean working tool link, an accurate PNG/JPG explanation, encryption limitations, decode workflow, capacity guidance, and common sharing mistakes in one reader-first guide.
