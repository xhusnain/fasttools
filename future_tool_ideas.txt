## SEO Strategy for Ranking #1

### 1. Shift Focus to Long-Tail
- Target niche calculators instead of broad terms like "Scientific Calculator".
- Target emerging trends (AI, Biohacking).

### 2. Technical SEO & Schema
- **JSON-LD Schema**: Use `SoftwareApplication` or `HowTo` schema so Google recognizes it as a functional tool.
- **Math Verification Block**: Display the formula used (e.g., `Total = Principal × (1+r)^n`) to build E-E-A-T.
- **Interactive Result Clusters**: Don't just show a number; show a table/breakdown (e.g., revenue over 12 months for churn).

### 3. Comparison Strategy
- Create "Comparison Calculators" (e.g., "Loan vs. Lease Calculator") for users further down the decision funnel.

### 4. Localization
- Target regional keywords (e.g., "Salary Tax Calculator Pakistan 2026").

### 5. Content Structure
- **The Tool**: Top of page (no scroll).
- **The Formula**: Clearly stated.
- **The Why**: 300-word explanation of importance.
- **FAQs**: Capture "People Also Ask" questions.
