## TOP 10 — Build Next (Ranked by Traffic + Revenue Potential)

### 1. [DONE] Home Affordability Calculator
- **Category**: finance-tools
- **Why**: "How much house can I afford" = one of the highest-searched finance queries. Bankrate, NerdWallet, Zillow all have this. CPC: $20-45 for mortgage keywords.
- **Features**: Income, debts, down payment, interest rate, taxes, insurance → max home price, monthly payment, DTI ratio.
- **Traffic**: Millions of monthly searches. Every homebuyer uses one.

### 2. [DONE] Auto Loan / Car Payment Calculator
- **Category**: finance-tools
- **Why**: "Car payment calculator" has massive volume. Auto loan CPC: $15-35. Every car buyer searches this.
- **Features**: Vehicle price, down payment, trade-in, interest rate, loan term → monthly payment, total interest, amortization.
- **Traffic**: Huge — people search before every car purchase.

### 3. [DONE] Rent vs Buy Calculator
- **Category**: finance-tools
- **Why**: "Should I rent or buy" is the most common housing question. High CPC ($15-40). Zillow, NerdWallet, Schwab all rank for this.
- **Features**: Rent amount, home price, mortgage rate, down payment, maintenance, appreciation → break-even year, 10-year comparison.
- **Traffic**: Evergreen, massive volume from first-time buyers.

### 4. [DONE] TDEE & Macro Calculator
- **Category**: calculator
- **Why**: "TDEE calculator" and "macro calculator" each have 500K+ monthly searches. Health CPC: $15-37. Fitness audience is enormous.
- **Features**: Age, gender, height, weight, activity level → TDEE, BMR, macros for cutting/bulking/maintenance, meal plan split.
- **Traffic**: 2M+ monthly combined. Fitness influencers share these constantly.

### 5. [DONE] Debt Payoff Calculator (Snowball & Avalanche)
- **Category**: finance-tools
- **Why**: "Debt payoff calculator" = high volume, high CPC ($20-40). Ramsey Solutions, Bankrate dominate. Personal finance is top AdSense niche.
- **Features**: Multiple debts input, snowball vs avalanche comparison, payoff timeline, total interest saved, extra payment impact.
- **Traffic**: Massive — anyone with credit card or student loan debt.

### 6. [DONE] Net Worth Calculator
- **Category**: finance-tools
- **Why**: "Net worth calculator" is searched by anyone doing financial planning. Finance CPC: $20-40.
- **Features**: Assets (cash, investments, property, vehicles, retirement) - liabilities (mortgage, loans, credit cards) = net worth with age benchmark.
- **Traffic**: High volume, highly shareable ("my net worth is $X").

### 7. 401k / Retirement Savings Calculator
- **Category**: finance-tools
- **Why**: "401k calculator" has huge search volume. Retirement planning CPC: $25-45. Every working adult needs this.
- **Features**: Current savings, monthly contribution, employer match, expected return, years to retirement → projected balance, income in retirement.
- **Traffic**: Enormous — evergreen demand from entire working population.

### 8. [DONE] Ovulation & Fertility Calculator
- **Category**: calculator
- **Why**: "Ovulation calculator" = millions of monthly searches globally. Health CPC: $15-35. Extremely sticky (users return monthly).
- **Features**: Last period date, cycle length → fertile window, ovulation day, next period, best days to conceive.
- **Traffic**: Massive recurring traffic — women check monthly.

### 9. Tax Bracket Calculator (US Federal + State)
- **Category**: finance-tools
- **Why**: "Tax bracket calculator" spikes every year (Jan-Apr) but has strong year-round volume. Tax CPC: $20-45.
- **Features**: Filing status, income, deductions → effective tax rate, marginal rate, tax owed, bracket breakdown, state comparison.
- **Traffic**: Huge seasonal spike + consistent base. Very high CPC.

### 10. Startup Runway Calculator
- **Category**: finance-tools
- **Why**: "Startup runway calculator" = high-intent B2B SaaS keyword. Pairs with our SaaS Churn + Payback tools. CPC: $15-30 for startup keywords.
- **Features**: Cash on hand, monthly burn rate, monthly revenue, growth rate → months of runway, zero-cash date, fundraise timing.
- **Traffic**: Lower volume than consumer tools but extremely high CPC and completes our SaaS cluster.

- **Category**: developer-tools
- **Why**: Extremely high dev search volume. "Cron expression generator" is searched by every backend developer. Sticky tool — users bookmark and return.
- **Features**: Visual cron builder with drag/drop, human-readable output, next 5 run times preview, common presets, reverse parsing (paste cron → explain).
- **Traffic**: 500K+ monthly dev searches. Low CPC but very high volume and repeat usage.

### 12. [DONE] Image Resizer
- **Category**: image-tools
- **Why**: Fills a major gap in our image tools lineup. "Resize image online" has massive search volume. Every content creator, marketer, and dev needs this.
- **Features**: Custom dimensions (px/%), aspect ratio lock, social media presets (Instagram, Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn), bulk resize, format conversion (JPG/PNG/WebP), drag-and-drop upload.
- **Traffic**: 2M+ monthly searches. Very high repeat usage.

### 13. AI Paraphraser / Rewriter
- **Category**: ai-tools
- **Why**: Trending AI tool with extremely sticky usage. Students, writers, marketers all use paraphrasers daily. "Paraphrasing tool" = massive volume.
- **Features**: Multiple rewrite modes (formal, casual, creative, academic), tone adjustment, plagiarism-safe output, side-by-side comparison, word count tracking.
- **Traffic**: 3M+ monthly searches. One of the highest-traffic AI tool categories.

### 14. Schema Markup Generator (JSON-LD)

- **Why**: Strong SEO niche tool. Every webmaster and SEO professional needs structured data. "Schema markup generator" = high-intent traffic.
- **Features**: Visual form builder for Article, Product, FAQ, HowTo, LocalBusiness, Event, Recipe schemas. Live JSON-LD preview, Google Rich Results validator link, copy-to-clipboard.
- **Traffic**: 200K+ monthly searches. High CPC ($10-25) from SEO/marketing audience.

### 15. DNS Lookup / SSL Checker
- **Category**: web-tools
- **Why**: High-traffic utility tools used by devs, sysadmins, and SEO professionals. "DNS lookup" and "SSL checker" have massive combined volume.
- **Features**: DNS records (A, AAAA, MX, CNAME, TXT, NS), SSL certificate details (expiry, issuer, chain), WHOIS info, response time, propagation check.
- **Traffic**: 1M+ monthly combined searches. Very high repeat usage from technical audience.
