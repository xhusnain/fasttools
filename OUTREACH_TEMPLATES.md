## 🎯 **BEST PRACTICES**

### **Email Personalization**
1. Research the recipient and their site
2. Mention specific content or achievements
3. Reference their audience and needs
4. Use their name and company name
5. Customize the value proposition

### **Timing and Frequency**
1. Send emails Tuesday-Thursday
2. Best times: 10 AM - 2 PM
3. Follow up after 1 week, 2 weeks, 1 month
4. Maximum 3 follow-ups
5. Respect unsubscribe requests

### **Value Proposition**
1. Focus on benefits, not features
2. Use specific numbers and statistics
3. Mention unique advantages
4. Provide social proof
5. Make it easy to say yes

### **Call to Action**
1. Be clear and specific
2. Make it easy to respond
3. Provide multiple options
4. Set expectations
5. Follow up appropriately
